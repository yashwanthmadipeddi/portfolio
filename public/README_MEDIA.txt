Media guide

Place your recorded demo videos in:
  public/videos/

Recommended names:
  career-connect.mp4
  fieldflow.mp4
  servicedesk.mp4
  project-04.mp4
  project-05.mp4

Recommended recording:
  MP4 / H.264
  1600x900 or 1920x1080
  15–30 seconds
  muted
  no audio required

The portfolio uses lazy-loaded looping videos. Until you add a video,
the project showcase displays a clean placeholder panel.
